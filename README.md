## Overview

This repository contains teaching materials, example prompts, exercises, and supporting resources for the course  
**“Large Language Models in Research and Science: Foundations, Tools, and Practice”**  
at Martin Luther University Halle-Wittenberg.

The course provides a **conceptual, critical, and hands-on introduction** to Large Language Models (LLMs) in scientific research. It focuses on how LLMs work, how they can be used productively in research workflows, and where their limitations and ethical risks lie.

---

## Course Structure

### Part I: Foundations & Concepts
- Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Generative AI, and LLMs
- Working principles of LLMs
- Typical scientific use cases
- Prompt engineering:

### Part II: Tools & Hands-On
- Knowledge mining with **Perplexity**
- Knowledge transformation with **ChatGPT**
- Knowledge organization with **NotebookLM**
